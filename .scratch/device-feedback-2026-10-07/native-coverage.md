## Agreed presentation decisions

- **Same voice:** shared verdict and hand-outcome narration now distinguish correctness from the submitted value; choice wording may remain first-person. Root reports the app-wide narration sweep complete at current HEAD; native language/accessibility review remains.
- **Portrait position box loop:** source has a 4×4 grid with eight occupied seats and direction arrows (`LessonSeatLoop`), replacing the oval teaching layout in those contexts. This is source evidence, not visual acceptance.
- **Bottom primary:** walkthrough navigation and drill answer/reveal sheet now use bottom safe-area insets. FirstLesson still uses a fixed `ActionSheet` after its scroll area. Confirm bottom actions are reachable and visually primary across small and accessibility-sized screens.
- **Matched normal EN/KO line counts:** not verifiable from paired string literals. `LessonPairedText` deliberately joins lines differently at accessibility size. Confirm matched *rendered* line counts only for the specified normal-size screens after native capture; do not state this as implemented from source.
