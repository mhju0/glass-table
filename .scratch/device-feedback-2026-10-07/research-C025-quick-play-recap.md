# C025 — Ten-hand Quick Play recap

User request: provide an overall recap after ten hands, allow reviewing all hands, and combine one casual general phrase with exact supporting data.

Research pending: inspect end-of-session navigation, retained hand histories, decision/action counts, chart applicability and replay. Candidate straightforward descriptions include call/raise/fold counts with explicitly named decision-opportunity denominators, number of hands reaching showdown, chip change and applicable chart matches. These are candidates, not confirmed available metrics.

Bluffing, luck and risk/safe labels need operational definitions and sufficient data. Do not infer bluff intention from an unsuccessful bet or call a winning outcome proof of sound play. Chart matches should name the beginner policy and eligible situations; ten hands are a small descriptive sample, not evidence of a stable playing style.

Proposed information hierarchy: one short session observation, a few numerical facts, all ten hand rows, and per-hand action/decision review. English/Korean and light/dark layouts remain for the final proposal report. No implementation during active testing.
