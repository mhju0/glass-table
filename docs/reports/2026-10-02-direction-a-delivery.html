The native redesign

Portrait seating, one card back and a fixed action dock give the table a stable frame. Cream identifies you; gold identifies the acting seat, your selection or the primary commit. The dock keeps its height across the decision, raise and Math faces.

Seats and cards
You sit bottom-right in normal portrait 2-, 3- and 4-player layouts. AX5 uses stacked seats with you first. Seats show name, one status slot and stack; commitments sit on the felt. Your cards are 43 pt, other table cards 36 pt.
Decision and raise
Fold, Check/Call and Raise/Bet keep fixed positions. Fold, Check and Call commit on release. Raise uses five presets, hold-to-repeat −/+ and a named commit button.
Math and review
계산 · 이번 핸드 · 규칙 / Math · This hand · Rules replace the help sheet. Math uses public facts. Review equity appears after the hand only when one live opponent’s cards were shown at showdown.
Motion and feedback
Paced deals, chip movement, refunds, all-in runout and pot settlement follow the replay state. Reduce Motion uses short crossfades; background/resume continues without stale feedback.
App-wide presentation
Shared spacing and radius roles, spade backs, paper position markers, outlined current replay steps and graded action slots. Named Korean bet actions use 벳.
Preserved boundaries
Engine, progression schema and saved settings remain unchanged. Grades retain decide-before-reveal. The app stays offline, without accounts, analytics, ads or real-money wagering.

Use a real hand to judge feedback feel, silence and collision handling:

  1. Selection tick when flip, tab, preset or −/+ changes. An unchanged preset and controls at their limits stay silent.
  2. Light computer-chip impact at 0.5, rising to 0.8 for all-in. Computer checks, folds and blinds stay silent.
  3. Soft card impact at 0.5 for public-card landing, showdown reveal and your card flip.
  4. Rigid impact at 0.75 once when the learner’s decision unlocks.
  5. Medium commit at 0.8; an all-in commit uses heavy impact with the same gold button treatment.
  6. Success for winning or sharing a pot. Losing stays silent.
  7. At least 150 ms between feedback events. Collision priority: your turn > commit > won > card > chip > tick. Background/resume must not replay stale feedback.

Real VoiceOver and older-runtime rendering still need separate checks. Store submission and existing distribution gates remain outside this delivery.
