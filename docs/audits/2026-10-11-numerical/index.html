Are the app’s numbers correct?

Core hand frequencies and poker arithmetic passed the independent checks. The audit nevertheless found 5 current teaching defects, 2 time-dependent display defects and 5 qualification/presentation items. They are listed below with concrete examples and proposed corrections. No app code, chart policy, saved data or installed version was changed by this audit.

Current issues and suggested corrections

All recommendations preserve correct calculations and existing learning/progress definitions. Changes to a strategy model need separate design and regression review.

N01 · Correct teaching

Zero EV is called a loss

Both guided languages say a call loses chips at EV = 0; the Korean answer reveal also says 손해. English answer reveal itself is neutral.

Check

Pot before bet 20bb; bet/call 10bb; equity 25%: 0.25 × 30 − 0.75 × 10 = 0bb.

Suggested correction
Use a third break-even branch: “The call breaks even on average.” / “평균적으로 본전인 콜이에요.” Preserve the EV and grade.

N02 · Correct teaching

Korean PFR explanation changes the denominator

“VPIP … 그중 PFR …%” suggests a share among participating hands, while VPIP and PFR in this model are both shares of all starting hands.

Check

Of 100 hands: enter 40, raise 10. PFR is 10% of all hands; raises among entries are 25%. The calling-band gap is 30 percentage points.

Suggested correction
“전체 핸드 중 40%로 참여하고, 전체 핸드 중 10%로 레이즈해요.” EN: “Enter 40% of all hands and raise 10% of all hands.” Use dynamic values.

N03 · Correct teaching

An outs lesson can start ahead or tied

The generator accepts a small count of winning rivers as a substitute for checking that the user is currently behind. Its walkthrough then asserts that the opponent leads.

Check

Seed 0xA0D17, index 35: Js4h versus 3hJh on 8d2sJcAc. Hero already leads by the 4 versus 3 kicker, yet the spot has 3 winning rivers. In the fixed 1,000-spot probe: 15 ahead and 22 tied. This is a probe rate, not an estimated population rate.

Suggested correction
For a “turn it around” lesson, require hero to be strictly behind before accepting a spot. Keep the correct river enumeration. Alternatively explicitly teach winning river cards without asserting a comeback.

N04 · Correct teaching

A tied river is described as losing

Excluded apparent flush cards are every card absent from strict winning outs, including ties. Current explanations say these leave the opponent ahead. Excluding ties from outright winning outs is correct; the reason is wrong.

Check

Seed 0xA0D17, index 116: Qc9c versus 2hJd on 4s9sJsKs. River 2s makes both players use the board-only K-high flush: a tie.

Suggested correction
Explain each result accurately: “This card ties the hand, so it is not an outright winning out.” / “이 카드는 무승부라서, 이기는 아웃에는 포함되지 않아요.” Separate tie and loss groups.

N05 · Correct teaching

Winning chance is used for tie-inclusive equity

The English Call/Fold reveal labels expected pot share “Winning chance.” The exact calculation correctly includes half of each heads-up tie. This is not strictly the probability of winning outright.

Check

If every outcome ties, strict winning probability is 0%, but equity is 50%. The call threshold must compare against equity.

Suggested correction
Use “Your share of the pot, including ties” / “무승부 몫을 포함한 팟 지분” or consistently explain equity. Keep the calculation and comparison.

N09 · Qualify

Sample uncertainty needs units and scope

Range advantage says “about ±1% error.” The 8,000-sample calculation has worst-case standard error about 0.559 percentage points, and an approximate 95% margin about 1.096 points. Neither is a guaranteed error bound.

Check

Sampling is deterministic for its seed; the result is still a statistical estimate. The confidence statement also depends on accepted sample count and sampling assumptions.

Suggested correction
Prefer “Estimated from 8,000 samples.” / “8,000회 표본으로 추정했어요.” If a margin is shown, say percentage points, confidence level and approximation.

Future-model risks, separate from current defects

Mixed range weights: range percentages support partial weights, but combo expansion drops those weights and equity/distribution APIs then treat expanded combos equally. A test range with AA weight 1 and 22 weight 0.01 incorrectly evaluates like equal-weight AA+22 in those APIs. Current published generated teaching ranges are pure0/1, so this is a future API risk, not evidence that current chart grades are wrong.

Bet facing a raise: one graded EV branch averages the best continuation separately for each hidden opponent combo. That would give the player information they do not have. Independent source review found the branch unreachable under all current published policies: checked-to hands exclude betting buckets and raising buckets are subsets of betting buckets. Preserve that invariant; before expanding policies, maximize against the aggregate observed raising range.

Interpretation checks to keep

One-card outs approximation is not two-card probability. With 9 fixed outs and 47 unseen cards, the next-card chance is 19.1489% and the chance to encounter an out across two cards is 34.9676%. In this app’s face-up-opponent turn drill, 44 rivers remain:9/44=20.4545% outright wins. A rule-of-2 estimate gives 18%, already labeled approximate. Include tie shares separately for equity. These numbers assume the stated fixed-out/card set and do not guarantee a final win after an opponent redraw.

Call pricing uses the pot after the opponent’s bet and before your call. For pot 20, bet 10, call 10, the final pot is 40 and break-even equity 25%. MDF instead uses the pot before the bet:20/(20+10)=66.6667% defense against a zero-showdown-value pure bluff in the one-bet model. A correct MDF number is not an instruction to call every hand or a replacement for the chart.

Progress reports practice evidence, not certified poker skill. Help remains the learner’s choice and stays included in approved aggregate practice totals. Style intervals describe recent play against these bots; the repeated observations are not guaranteed independent. A 90% calibration target is the stated interval coverage target, not proof that the app teaches 90% of material or that a user remembers 90%.
