### Rules, models and scope

D7 uses TDA no-limit action and reopening rules. The worked short-raise case is bet 10, all-in to 14, third player calls. The initial bettor cannot re-raise since the action returned with an increase of only 4. The contrasting full raise is 11 to 22, with chips behind in another seat. D8 settles each eligible pool separately and returns an unmatched excess. Cumulative short raises, odd chips, and live rule variation remain outside the assessed batch.

M1 addresses heads-up effective stacks rather than using the shortest stack as a universal cap on a multiway hand. M2 supplies the **joint** both-fold probability, without assuming independent folds. M3 gives an opponent response model so the exercise can isolate the effect of stronger and weaker calls. M4 uses fixed cash blinds and a freezeout bubble as its examples. It does not prescribe a tournament action from chip EV alone. N1 and N3 use chip EV for a heads-up all-in under stated cards, sufficient chips, no fees and no further betting. The turn threshold is the call divided by the pot **after** calling. N2 separates that earlier calculation from the actual river result. These strategy choices illustrate declared models. The app uses simulated chips.

## Learner timing protocol and remaining batches

Use at least six novice adults, include both languages and a mix of small/default and enlarged-text phones. Begin with an unprompted first-run task. Record question ID, first answer, confidence, whether the learner used help, explanation or retry time, and arrival-to-resume time. For a reopened draft/reveal, verify the exact card evidence and selection before continuing. Capture per-item active start and stop in the observer log. Pause the clock when the app is backgrounded, the learner stops or an interruption occurs. Time reading, thinking, calculations, answer entry and feedback. Collect completion, median and interquartile active time by language and item type, and mark incomplete paths separately. Keep only consented, anonymized notes outside the app.

At course end and again 2–4 days later give unseen deals. Before reveal, ask the learner for the order of action, a legal size, made hand versus draw, price reasoning, and best five. Score each one independently with alternate valid reasoning accepted in the observer rubric. Compare unaided first accuracy, correction recovery and delayed transfer. A correct fold should retain credit if a later card would have won. Record fast versus slow cohorts and revise the time estimate before using it in product copy.

The candidate supplies the quizzes and review bank. Remaining batches are learner timing and qualitative validation, the separate item 31 B10 onboarding and resume treatment, and deeper tournament procedures or richer free-text and branching assessment if research supports them.
