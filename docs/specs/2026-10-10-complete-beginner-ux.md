# Approved beginner UX completion

Owner approved all three remaining recommendations on October 10: five required
intro comparisons, freely available chart with a dismissible invitation after
several correct decisions, and an optional replayable guided hand after fundamentals.

## Native implementation

- Required intro progresses pair versus high card, higher pair, trips, straight,
  flush. Rules appear before choices; Previous/Skip remain. Curated three-example
  banks vary ranks/card use and alternate ownership without changing saved drills.
- Basics and learning-guide retrieval examples rotate meaningful situations.
- Beginner Defend explains chart/actions first; chart remains visible/reopenable.
  Three successfully committed on-target choices in a live practice view offer
  one dismissible invitation. No public help categories or forced removal; the
  invitation is transient and does not rewrite existing assistance semantics.
- Graded reference starts with expanded reading guidance and plain action labels.
  Selected-hand recommendations remain hidden until commitment.
- Restored positions use numbered plain seats without a Dynamic Type cap. Restored
  ranges retain estimate outlines/matched/missing/extra evidence; restored outs
  group equivalent outcomes. Legacy and band inputs use the unchanged grader.
- Equity copy states wins plus the learner's share of ties in both languages,
  including primary questions, walkthroughs and restored context. Grading unchanged.
- Optional guided hand uses local legal seeded deals, call/check/rule questions,
  commitment before feedback, explicit demonstration actions, chip conservation,
  a less-guided replay and ordinary Play routing. Learn always offers replay;
  basics completion offers an optional continuation. It does not mutate saved
  lessons/tables, earn mastery, or claim demonstrated actions are optimal strategy.
- Normal controls stay at the bottom; AX content/actions use continuous scrolling.

## Boundaries and acceptance

No Engine changes, persistence/schema changes, credit rules, grading thresholds,
active seeds, archive/recovery changes or release submission. The local guided
hand intentionally restarts after closing; real lessons and games retain their
existing save/resume behavior. Portfolio video and real no-poker participant
research remain external evidence tasks.

Build/package gates and focused native interaction/capture outcomes are recorded
in the delivery report after execution. Source presence is not native acceptance.
Check EN/KO × light/dark normal/AX5, actual default intro finish, voluntary chart
collapse/reopen, wrong/right guided choices/back/replay, restored range/position,
help finish/back/close and safe bottoms. Every requested owner reinstall resets
data unless explicitly asked to preserve; never copy phone progress off-device.
